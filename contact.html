JOIN MUST GREEN

MUST Green is a student-led tree planting and environmental conservation campaign dedicated to creating a greener, healthier and more sustainable Meru University campus.

By joining MUST Green, students can participate in tree planting activities, learn about different tree species, promote environmental awareness and contribute to a campus where nature and university life grow together.

Become a Member

Joining MUST Green gives students an opportunity to take practical action towards environmental conservation. Members can participate in tree planting, campus clean-up activities, environmental awareness campaigns and other sustainability initiatives.

You do not need to be an environmental science student to participate. Every student can contribute skills, ideas, time and energy towards building a greener university.

How You Can Get Involved

  • Participate in tree planting activities.
  • Help care for and protect young trees.
  • Take part in campus clean-up activities.
  • Learn and share information about different tree species.
  • Promote environmental awareness among students.
  • Suggest new ideas for improving the university environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join MUST Green?

Any student who is interested in environmental conservation, tree planting and creating a greener campus can participate.

Do I need previous environmental experience?

No. Students can learn through participation and teamwork. The most important requirement is a willingness to contribute positively to the environment.

What activities does MUST Green organise?

Activities may include tree planting, environmental awareness, campus clean-ups, biodiversity conservation and educational activities.

Be Part of the Change

A greener campus begins with individual action. Whether you plant one tree, protect an existing tree, participate in a clean-up or educate another student, your contribution matters.
