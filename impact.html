Understanding Our Impact

Environmental impact refers to the changes that activities can create in the natural environment and in the communities that depend on it.

MUST Green aims to create positive environmental change through tree planting, conservation, environmental education, responsible waste management and active student participation.

Impact of Tree Planting

Trees play an important role in maintaining healthy ecosystems. They provide habitats for organisms, help protect soil, improve the appearance of the campus and contribute to a healthier environment.

Planting and caring for trees also contributes to long-term environmental conservation by increasing vegetation cover and supporting biodiversity.

Biodiversity Conservation

Biodiversity refers to the variety of living organisms found in an environment, including plants, animals, insects and microorganisms.

By promoting tree planting and protecting green spaces, MUST Green supports habitats that can contribute to the survival and diversity of different organisms.

Waste Management Impact

Responsible waste management helps reduce pollution and promotes cleaner surroundings within the university community.

MUST Green encourages responsible disposal, waste reduction, recycling and greater awareness of the effects of poor waste management on the environment.

Environmental Education

Environmental education equips students and members of the university community with knowledge about environmental challenges and sustainable solutions.

Through awareness campaigns, discussions and practical activities, students can develop knowledge and skills that encourage responsible environmental behaviour.

Student Participation and Leadership

Students are an important part of creating sustainable environmental change. Their participation allows environmental ideas to be transformed into practical activities.

Participation in MUST Green activities can also help students develop teamwork, leadership, communication and community engagement skills.

Key Areas of Impact

  • Biodiversity conservation
  • Responsible waste management
  • Environmental awareness and education
  • Student participation and leadership
  • Community environmental responsibility

Measuring Our Progress

Measuring environmental progress helps an organisation understand whether its activities are producing meaningful results.

Some useful indicators can include the number of trees planted, the number of students participating in activities, the amount of waste collected and the number of environmental awareness activities organised.

  • Number of trees planted
  • Number of students participating
  • Number of environmental events organised
  • Amount of waste collected or recycled
  • Number of environmental awareness activities

Our Future Environmental Goals

MUST Green aims to continue encouraging environmental responsibility and expanding conservation activities within the university community.

Future activities can focus on increasing tree planting, improving waste management, protecting biodiversity, strengthening environmental education and encouraging more students to participate in sustainability initiatives.

Make an Impact

Every environmental action matters. By participating in MUST Green activities, students and members of the university community can contribute to a cleaner, greener and more sustainable environment.
