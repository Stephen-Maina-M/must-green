About Our Environmental Events

MUST Green events provide opportunities for students, staff and members of the university community to participate in practical environmental conservation activities.

Through these activities, participants develop environmental awareness, teamwork, leadership skills and a sense of responsibility towards the environment.

Our Major Events

Tree Planting Day

Tree Planting Day brings together students and members of the university community to plant and care for trees around the campus and other suitable areas.

The activity contributes to campus greening, biodiversity conservation, soil protection and the improvement of the surrounding environment.

Environmental Awareness Campaign

This campaign promotes awareness of environmental challenges such as pollution, deforestation, poor waste management and loss of biodiversity.

Students can participate through awareness talks, educational activities, discussions and information-sharing sessions.

Campus Clean-Up Day

Campus Clean-Up Day encourages students and staff to work together to maintain clean, healthy and attractive university surroundings.

The activity promotes responsible waste disposal and encourages the university community to take ownership of its environment.

Biodiversity Conservation Day

Biodiversity Conservation Day focuses on understanding and protecting plants, trees, insects, birds and other organisms that contribute to a healthy ecosystem.

Participants learn about the importance of maintaining biodiversity and protecting natural habitats.

How Students Can Participate

  • Participate in tree planting activities.
  • Take part in campus clean-up activities.
  • Attend environmental awareness sessions.
  • Support biodiversity conservation activities.
  • Encourage other students to practise environmental responsibility.

Why Our Events Matter

Environmental events transform environmental awareness into practical action. They allow members of the university community to contribute directly to conservation and sustainability.

They also provide opportunities for students to develop teamwork, leadership, communication and community engagement skills.

Be Part of the Change

Environmental conservation is a shared responsibility. Join MUST Green and take part in activities that help create a cleaner, greener and more sustainable university environment.
